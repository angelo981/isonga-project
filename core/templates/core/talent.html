The Journey

How Growth Happens
at ISONGA

Every talent who comes through ISONGA moves through a deliberate, supported pathway from raw potential to professional progress.

🔍
Step 1

Discover

Identify your passion, enter the space, and meet the team through open auditions or intake.

📚
Step 2

Train

Attend structured sessions with facilitators who guide your skill development.

🔄
Step 3

Practice

Rehearse, iterate, and build real-world confidence through repetition and feedback.

🎬
Step 4

Produce

Create tangible work — recordings, content, performances — with Centre support.

🎤
Step 5

Perform

Take the stage, present to an audience, and earn real exposure in the community.

🚀
Step 6

Progress

Launch a career, secure partnerships, and continue growing beyond ISONGA.
