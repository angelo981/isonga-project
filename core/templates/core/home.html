Our Purpose

Why ISONGA
Centre Exists

Rwanda's creative economy is growing fast, but many young people and emerging artists still lack access to professional spaces, structured mentorship, and market connections.

ISONGA CENTRE was created to close that gap. Based in Musanze, we provide an accessible platform where youth and creatives can learn, perform, connect, and build their future.
