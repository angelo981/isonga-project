About ISONGA CENTRE

A flagship initiative of TOP5SAI Ltd — a hybrid creative, cultural, and youth empowerment hub rooted in Musanze, Rwanda, built to turn potential into progress.

Our Story

Who We Are

ISONGA CENTRE exists to provide young people and emerging creatives with access to training, mentorship, production support, and performance opportunities in a professional, inspiring, and community-driven environment.

We are more than a venue — we are a movement. A place where Rwanda's next generation of artists, entrepreneurs, and innovators come to find their voice, sharpen their craft, and step into their future.

Based in Musanze, we draw on the full expertise and infrastructure of the TOP5SAI to deliver world-class programs at the local level — accessible to all.

Our Vision

Rwanda's Leading Creative & Cultural Hub

To become Rwanda's leading creative and cultural hub where youth talent is discovered, nurtured, and transformed into sustainable careers — driving innovation, cultural expression, and economic growth for generations to come.

🎯

Our Mission

Empowering Youth Through Creativity

To empower youth and creatives through skills development, talent incubation, and dynamic cultural programming — by leveraging TOP5SAI's expertise, infrastructure, and industry networks to create opportunities, inspire creativity, and build sustainable career pathways.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Everything we do — every program, performance, and partnership — is guided by six principles that define who we are and how we operate.

💡

Creativity & Innovation

We encourage bold thinking, artistic expression, and new ideas that push boundaries and open doors.

⭐

Excellence & Professionalism

We strive for quality in every program, production, and experience — setting the standard for creative education in Rwanda.

🤝

Empowerment & Inclusion

We create opportunities for youth, women, and underserved groups — ensuring no talented person is left behind.

🛡️

Integrity & Accountability

We value trust, transparency, and responsible leadership in everything from finances to community relations.

🌐

Collaboration & Partnership

Cultural Identity & Heritage
